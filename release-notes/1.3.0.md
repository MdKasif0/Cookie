Cookie 1.3.0

Cookie becomes a companion you grow with: a friendship that unlocks things, a helper for focused work, and a few cozy places of her own.

New
- Friendship: your bond with Cookie grows from the affection you give her and the days you come back, through five levels from Just Met to Inseparable. Each level unlocks new accessories, coats, toys and cozy spots, and she celebrates every one
- Focus sessions (Focus Session in the menu): Cookie naps beside you while you work, then stretches and nudges you to take a break
- A daily rhythm: she greets you by time of day, dresses for Halloween, the holidays and winter, and wears a party hat on her birthday. You can turn these off in Settings
- A cozy corner (Cozy Corner in the menu): a cat bed, a sunbeam spot and a scratching post that she visits on her own. Drag them where you like
- Photo mode (Photo Mode… in the menu): save Cookie as a PNG or a looping GIF, or copy her, with ten poses and six backdrops
- Presence settings: Cookie hides while a full-screen app or presentation is in front, and asks macOS to leave her out of screen sharing and recordings (this works with many capture tools, but macOS 15 and later can ignore the request for some). Press Control-Option-C to call her to your cursor, or pick another shortcut in Settings

Changed
- Some toys, coats and accessories now unlock as your bond grows. Anything you already use stays available
