Cookie 1.4.0 for Windows

The biggest Windows update yet: Cookie now moves, looks and sounds like she does on the Mac, grows closer to you over time, and helps with your day. Anything that needs a permission or goes online stays off until you turn it on.

New since 1.1.0
- Real animation: walking, running, sleeping, eating, stretching and more, drawn frame by frame from the same artwork as the Mac version
- A Windows 11 look: Fluent styling, Mica, and light and dark mode that follow Windows
- A new welcome, redrawn toys, treats and accessories, new menu icons, and warmer sounds with a purr that carries on while you pet her
- Friendship: your bond grows from the affection you give her and the days you come back, and each level unlocks accessories, coats, toys and cozy spots
- Focus sessions, a daily rhythm with seasonal outfits, a cozy corner with a bed, a sunbeam and a scratching post, and Photo Mode
- Presence: Cookie steps aside for full-screen apps and games, stays out of screen sharing, and Ctrl+Alt+C calls her to your cursor
- Reminders and quick timers: she comes to your cursor and meows when it's time, even over a full-screen app
- Break nudges for your eyes, posture and water, a focus guard for distracting apps, and a bedtime wind-down (all off until you choose them)
- A system watcher for low battery, a busy CPU, a nearly full disk and dropped Wi-Fi
- Sticky notes on your desktop, and a to-do list where finished tasks earn bond points
- Download alerts and a tidy-up that moves old Desktop screenshots into Pictures\Screenshots (both off until you turn them on; nothing is deleted)
- A quiet mode you can switch on for a while, and cookie:// links for Command Prompt, PowerShell, Power Automate and shortcuts
- Drop files on Cookie to move, copy or zip them, an optional clipboard history kept in memory only, and an optional chat with Cookie using your own Claude API key

Changed
- Updates are now signed, and Cookie checks the signature before trusting an update
- Settings has new Helpers and Power Tools tabs
- Cookie now runs on .NET 10

Not on Windows yet
- Calendar heads-ups: Windows only lets apps from the Microsoft Store read your calendar
