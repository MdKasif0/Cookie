Cookie 1.4.0

Cookie learns to help: she reminds you of things, looks after your breaks and your Mac, keeps notes and a to-do list, and can be driven from Shortcuts. Anything that needs a macOS permission or goes online stays off until you turn it on.

New
- Reminders and timers (Reminders in the menu): "Remind me in 20 minutes to call Mom". When it's time, Cookie comes to your cursor, meows and tells you, even over a full-screen app
- Break nudges (Settings → Helpers, off until you choose them): rest your eyes every 20 minutes, check your posture every 45, drink water every hour. Only time at the keyboard counts, and she waits during focus sessions, video calls and full-screen apps
- Focus guard: choose your distracting apps, and during a focus session Cookie comes over and gives you a look if you open one. She can see which app is in front, never which website
- Bedtime (off until you turn it on): once a night she yawns, suggests wrapping up and curls up to sleep
- System watcher: she lets you know when your battery is low, your Mac has been maxed out for a few minutes, your disk is nearly full, or the internet drops and comes back
- Calendar heads-up (off until you turn it on, and macOS asks for calendar access): "Standup in 5 minutes"
- Sticky notes (Sticky Notes in the menu): pin notes on your desktop beside Cookie or her bed. Drag them anywhere, recolor them, edit them, or tick them off
- To-do list (To-Do in the menu): each task you finish earns bond points, up to five a day
- Downloads and screenshots (both off until you turn them on): she tells you when a download finishes, and offers to tidy old screenshots into a Screenshots folder. Nothing is deleted
- Shortcuts and links: Cookie's actions are in the Shortcuts app, and links like cookie://say?text=Hello or cookie://focus/start?minutes=25 work from Terminal, Raycast or scripts
- Quiet mode (Quiet Mode in the menu): hide Cookie for 30 minutes, an hour, two hours or until you turn it off. Reminders still get through
- File drop zone: drag files onto Cookie to move or copy them to a folder you choose, or zip them. Nothing is overwritten
- Clipboard buddy (off until you turn it on): your last 10 copies, kept in memory only and forgotten when Cookie quits. Password manager copies are skipped
- Ask Cookie (off until you turn it on): chat with Cookie using your own Claude API key. This is the only feature that goes online; your questions are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, and the chat is forgotten when you close its window

Changed
- Settings has two new tabs, Helpers and Power Tools, and the Stats tab counts the tasks you finish
