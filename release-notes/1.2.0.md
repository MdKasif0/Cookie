Cookie 1.2.0

A big polish release: Cookie moves better, looks more like herself everywhere, and sounds warmer.

New
- Real animation: walking, running, sleeping, eating, waking, stretching and more are now drawn frame by frame from Cookie's own artwork instead of being bent from a single picture
- A new welcome: Cookie walks in, reacts as you type her name, and each personality is an animated card
- Dark Mode throughout, and Liquid Glass on macOS 26
- Toys, treats and accessories are re-drawn as soft 3D art to match Cookie, and the cardboard box now sits around her
- A custom Cookie menu bar icon and menu icons, replacing the emoji
- Redesigned sounds with alternate takes, and a continuous purr that carries on while you pet her

Fixed
- Hats, glasses and other accessories now sit on Cookie's head and neck instead of floating away from her
- Toys and treats now have their shadows underneath them
