Indie Privacy Guarantee

Privacy is non-negotiable.

A desktop companion should make your workspace calmer and more personal — never report back on what you do.

  • Zero Telemetry: No tracking SDKs, analytics, or behavioral diagnostics are ever recorded.
  • 100% Offline: Cookie makes zero internet connections. It functions completely without a network.
  • Local Storage Only: All configuration (fur colors, toys, sound levels, and names) is saved locally via macOS UserDefaults or Windows %APPDATA%\Cookie\profile.json.
  • Open Codebase: Cookie is MIT-licensed open source software.
