Installation Instructions

Setting up Cookie takes less than ten seconds:

1

Download Cookie

Click the Download Cookie button above to download the Cookie-1.0.0.dmg disk image installer directly to your Mac.

2

Open the DMG

Locate the downloaded file in your Downloads folder (or Safari downloads list) and double-click to mount it.

3

Drag Cookie into Applications

In the installer window, drag the Cookie icon onto the Applications folder shortcut to copy the application to your Mac.

4

Open Cookie

Eject the disk image and open Cookie from your Applications folder, Launchpad, or Spotlight search (⌘ Space).

First Launch

What happens on first launch

  • 🐾
    Desktop Appearance: Cookie appears right on your wallpaper and gently takes her first steps across your display.
  • 🖥️
    Menu Bar Companion: A tiny cat silhouette icon sits discreetly in your macOS menu bar. Click it anytime to open Preferences, toggle sound, send Cookie to sleep, or customize appearance.
  • 🔒
    100% Local & Private: Cookie does not ask for network permissions, accounts, or telemetry. All settings are saved locally in your user preferences.
Direct Distribution

A note on direct distribution & macOS Gatekeeper

Cookie is an indie desktop app distributed directly from the developer rather than through the Mac App Store.

Standard macOS Security Note

Because Cookie is downloaded outside the App Store, macOS Gatekeeper may display a standard prompt on first launch:

“Cookie is an app downloaded from the internet. Are you sure you want to open it?”

Simply click Open to proceed. If your Mac requires confirmation under stricter security policies:

  1. In Finder, open your Applications folder.
  2. Control-click (or right-click) Cookie and select Open.
  3. Click Open in the confirmation prompt (or approve in System Settings → Privacy & Security).

Cookie is 100% open source under the MIT license. You can inspect every line of the Swift codebase and build it yourself from source on GitHub.
