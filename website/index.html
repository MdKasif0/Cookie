01 / Desktop Companion

Cookie is always there.

Lives quietly on your desktop, keeping you company while you write, design, code, and browse.

No browser tabs to keep pinned, no intrusive dock notifications. Cookie wanders gently across your screen, perches quietly beside active windows, and stays with you throughout your workday.

Indie Privacy Guarantee

Privacy is non-negotiable.

A desktop companion should make your workspace calmer and more personal — never report back on what you do.

  • Zero Telemetry: No tracking SDKs, analytics, or behavioral diagnostics are ever recorded.
  • 100% Offline: Cookie makes zero internet connections. It functions completely without a network.
  • Local Storage Only: All configuration (fur colors, toys, sound levels, and names) is saved locally via macOS UserDefaults.
  • Open Codebase: Cookie is MIT-licensed open source Swift software.
