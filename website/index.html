A quiet presence on your desktop.

Cookie isn’t a game with scores, and she isn’t another productivity app demanding your attention. She is simply a companion who keeps you company while you write, design, code, and browse.

01

Gentle Company

Cookie naps in warm corners of your display, stretches after long stretches of silence, and looks up with gentle curiosity whenever your cursor glides past.

02

Zero Interruptions

Never plays unexpected loud sounds during meetings, never sends push notifications, and never asks for a review. When you need absolute stillness, she rests quietly.

03

Handcrafted for Mac

Built strictly with Swift and SpriteKit. When she falls asleep, CPU usage drops to virtually 0%, preserving every drop of battery life on MacBooks.
